# Hi, I'm Rodrigo 👋

**Embedded firmware & electronics engineer** — I build reliable firmware for
microcontroller-based devices, with a focus on the hard cases: hardware with no
documentation, fail-safe behavior, and real-world reliability.

My background is in **medical and dental equipment**, where a bug isn't just a bug.

### What I do
- 🔧 Firmware in C/C++ for **STM32** and **AVR / LGT8F328P**
- 📡 Sensor & peripheral integration: **I²C, UART, SPI, ADC**, Bluetooth (BLE)
- 🩺 Medical-device automation and retrofits
- 🔌 Custom **PCB design** (EasyEDA / KiCad), SMD & through-hole
- 🛡️ Fail-safe, watchdog-backed, non-blocking designs

### Featured projects
